# Code of Conduct

Nitidoc is a small project. This document is short on purpose.

## The standard

Everyone is welcome here regardless of experience, background, nationality,
gender, identity, age, disability or beliefs. Contributions are judged on their
merits — never on who wrote them.

Concretely, what is expected of everybody taking part:

- **Be direct and be kind.** Blunt technical criticism is fine and useful.
  Contempt, mockery and personal attacks are not. Review the code, not the
  person who wrote it.
- **Assume good faith.** Most bad pull requests are a misunderstanding, not
  malice. Ask before you accuse.
- **Accept a "no".** Not every idea fits the project, and maintainers may
  decline a contribution. A rejected patch is not a rejected person.
- **Respect people's time.** Search before opening a duplicate. Fill in the
  issue template. Nobody here is being paid to reproduce a vague report.
- **Never post other people's private information**, and be careful with
  screenshots — this is a document scanner, so crop or blur anything real
  before attaching it.

Harassment, sexualized language or imagery, sustained disruption and any form of
discrimination are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests, discussions or
commit messages.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub — open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/santiagoisra/nitidoc/security/advisories/new)
if you need a confidential channel, or contact
[@santiagoisra](https://github.com/santiagoisra) directly.

Reports are handled discreetly. Depending on severity the response ranges from
a private warning to editing or removing the offending content, up to blocking
the account from the repository. The maintainer's decision is final.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — the repository, issues, pull requests and
discussions — and to anyone representing the project in public.
